# Beat Bop Doodle

Paint, make music, animate and play, in the spirit of classic paint-and-music games.

**Play:** https://majoraspride.github.io/beat-bop-doodle/

## Add it to your phone like an app
- **Android (Chrome):** open the link, tap the ⋮ menu, then **Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**).
- **iPhone (Safari):** open the link, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.

Your drawings and songs save on your own device.
